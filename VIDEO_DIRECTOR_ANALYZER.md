# Video Director Analyzer V1

Browser-based frame-by-frame technical analyzer for AI-generated video tests.

Open `video-director-analyzer.html`, choose an MP4, and press **SCAN VIDEO**.

V1 scans every frame at an assumed 30 FPS and reports abrupt visual changes, possible frozen sections, dark/fade frames, average brightness, and exact timestamps. It includes **COPY FULL REPORT** and **COPY CHATGPT REPORT** buttons.

V1 intentionally does not pretend to understand acting, characters, props, or story semantics yet. That is planned as a later layer.
